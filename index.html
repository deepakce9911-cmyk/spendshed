Questions

The ones every finance lead asks.

What do you need from us to start?

Three months of card statements, as PDF or CSV exports from your bank or card provider. That alone gives us most subscriptions. Connecting Gmail adds receipts for tools paid by individuals, and Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 adds login data so we can count idle seats.

Is our data safe?

Files are read once to build the report and stored encrypted until you delete them. Inbox and workspace access is read-only and can be revoked by you at any time. We never see passwords, and we never log in to your tools.

Will you cancel something we need?

No. Every cancellation, downgrade or negotiation is a proposed action you approve. Nothing moves without your yes, and you get the full email thread for each one.

How do you negotiate?

With usage data and timing. A vendor whose renewal is in four days, facing a customer with 19 idle seats, moves quickly. We handle the back and forth and send you the new terms to accept.

How is "savings" counted for the 25% plan?

The difference between what you were paying before an action and what you pay after it, for twelve months. We invoice after the lower bill shows up, not before.

What size of company is this for?

Teams of roughly 10 to 300 people. Below that, there isn't enough software to matter. Above that, you probably have a procurement team and tools like Vendr or Zip.
